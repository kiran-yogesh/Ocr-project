The Innovation behind Chef AI

The Problem

Household food waste contributes significantly to global emissions. Most waste occurs due to "invisible" inventory—ingredients ignored simply because they aren't tracked.

The Solution

Chef AI bridges this gap by creating a real-time digital twin of your kitchen. It provides actionable intelligence by suggesting recipes that maximize current inventory utility.

Core Technologies Used:
Python Flask SpaCy NLP Sentence Transformers Paddle OCR SQLite3 Scikit-Learn inflect rapidfuzz

Data-Driven Decisions

Chef AI analyzes your actual purchase history to suggest recipes that use what you already have, saving you time and money while reducing environmental impact through smarter consumption.
