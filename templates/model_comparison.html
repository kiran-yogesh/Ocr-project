Metric Dictionary

F1 Score

Harmonic mean of precision and recall — overall classification quality.

NDCG@K

Ranking quality — gives more weight to relevant results at the top.

MRR

Mean Reciprocal Rank — average rank position of the first relevant result.

Hit Rate@K

% of queries where at least one relevant recipe appears in the top K.

Catalog Coverage

% of total recipes the model can recommend with the current inventory.

FWRI

Food Waste Reduction Index — efficiency in surfacing near-expiry items.
