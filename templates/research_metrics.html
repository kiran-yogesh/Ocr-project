Metric Dictionary

F1 Overall

The harmonic mean of precision and recall. It represents the overall classification accuracy of the model.

Catalog Coverage

Measures the percentage of the total recipe database that the model is capable of recommending.

FWRI

Food Waste Reduction Index. A custom metric measuring efficiency in prioritizing ingredients nearing expiry.

CER

Character Error Rate. Measures individual character flips (e.g., 'S' vs '5') in OCR processing.

WER

Word Error Rate. Measures if whole words (like "Milk" or "Butter") are read correctly on bills.

KIE

Key Information Extraction. Evaluates if the AI correctly identified "Items", "Prices", or "Quantities".

Match Rate

The percentage of extracted text successfully mapped to valid database ingredients via fuzzy matching logic.
